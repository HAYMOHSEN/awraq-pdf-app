Privacy Policy

Awraq PDF – Arabic & English PDF Tools · Effective 4 October 2026 · Developer: Hani Muhsen

Awraq PDF processes your documents on your own computer. It never uploads, stores or shares them, has no account, and contains no advertising or tracking.

1. Who we are

Awraq PDF ("the app") is developed and published by Hani Muhsen, an independent developer based in Amman, Jordan. You can reach us at haymohsen@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account and do not collect your name, email address or any other personal information.
  • We do not collect the contents of your files, file names, or anything typed or edited inside the app.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or any other tracking technology.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any data, because we hold none.

3. How your files are handled

Every tool in Awraq PDF — merging, splitting, organizing, compressing, converting, adding page numbers or watermarks, PDF to Word, and OCR — runs inside the app on your PC. Your files are read into the app's memory only while a tool is working, and the result is written only to the location you choose in the Save dialog. Nothing is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

4. Data kept on your device

The app stores a small amount of information locally so it can work and remember your choices:

  • your interface language (Arabic or English);
  • whether Awraq Pro is unlocked on this PC;
  • a cached copy of the app's own files so that it can start without an internet connection;
  • after your first use of OCR, a cached copy of the text-recognition engine and its Arabic and English language data.

This information never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

5. When the app uses the internet

  • Loading the app. Awraq PDF is a web-based app delivered through the Microsoft Store. On first launch, and when an update is published, it downloads its own files from our hosting provider, GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may record standard connection logs (such as your IP address) under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • OCR engine. The first time you use the OCR tool, the app downloads the open-source Tesseract text-recognition engine and language files from the jsDelivr content delivery network, governed by the jsDelivr privacy policy. This request contains no part of your documents; recognition itself happens on your PC.
  • Purchases. Awraq Pro is bought through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft handles payment and your account under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; the app only receives confirmation that the purchase exists.

The core tools work entirely offline once the app has been installed.

6. Children

Awraq PDF is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

7. Security

The strongest protection for your documents is that they never leave your computer. The app requests no permissions beyond opening the files you choose and saving where you choose.

8. Your choices and rights

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. To remove the data the app keeps on your device, uninstall it. If you have any question about this policy, email haymohsen@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, we will update this page and the effective date at the top before the change takes effect.
